## Proposed method: PaRC (Pairwise-Relational Calibration via low-rank Bradley–Terry field)
- Short vLLM-batchable PAIRWISE prompt: "given history H, is item A or B more likely the next interaction?" → symmetrized logit s_ij = ½(logit(A,B) − logit(B,A)).
- Model preferences as a Bradley–Terry field s_ij ≈ θ_i − θ_j + ε_ij; decompose θ_i = α·(pony pointwise posterior_i) + β_i. HEADLINE OBJECT = β (the comparative correction that exists only under forced comparison) — claimed to be the part of the ordering INVISIBLE to any pointwise scorer.
- Recover the full order from O(K log K) ADAPTIVE duels (dueling-bandit/merge-sort schedule concentrated near the pony-induced top-k boundary), anchoring θ at pony's posterior. ~600–1300 short prompts/user (fewer total tokens than CC-PACE).
- Floor = pony's score (via α-anchor); claim: anchor + comparative correction ≥ anchor.
- RESEARCH QUESTION: does a frozen LLM's forced pairwise comparison carry ranking info PROVABLY ABSENT from its pointwise posterior (measurable as significant cyclic INTRANSITIVITY / non-BT residual), recoverable from O(K log K) duels to lift top-k NDCG at fixed compute?
